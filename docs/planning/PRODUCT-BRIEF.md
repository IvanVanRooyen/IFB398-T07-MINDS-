# Product scope

MINDS connects exploration/mining knowledge with its geographical and reporting context. Its core requirements are a central document/data workspace, spatial investigation, evidence-assisted discovery and traceable report preparation/review.

## Users and work

Geologists and field/data leads need to inspect projects, drillholes, measurements and technical documents together. Authors need to distinguish observations from hypotheses, assemble evidence and retain the inputs behind a report. Reviewers need a fixed revision with its supporting data rather than a view that silently follows changing live records.

The shared organisation, role and clearance policy applies across these flows. Platform administration is separate from ordinary application administration.

## Workflows

- Import and validate project data, preserving source coordinate provenance and document versions.
- Investigate geography, link evidence to a prospect and record its hypothesis/objective.
- Retrieve accessible records and inspect excerpts before accepting a draft.
- Prepare narrative manually or with optional AI; add bounded database-backed tables/figures.
- Save revisions, review an exact snapshot and export the evidence-bearing result.

The original university/client scope also included exploration and mining/operational modes, report assembly and observability. These remain part of the product direction. A coherent operational example must be defined before adding mining-specific entities or claiming mode completeness.

## Boundaries

The current appendix is not a complete annual-report engine. OCR, autonomous prospect scoring, formal framework certification and live coediting are not delivered capabilities. JORC/VALMIN-specific templates and requirements need domain review. AI output is assistance, not a source of verified geological findings.
